# Release 1: what each activity asks of her

This is the Release 1 catalogue from [the v1.0 research and product specification](LAYLA_BOARD_SPEC_V1_RESEARCH.md) (§8.4): 21 activities across ten areas, built to the mechanics in §7. The research's key point (§4.2) is that tapping alone is not learning. Each activity here asks her to **decide, predict or plan** something, and answers with an interesting result rather than a score.

## Shared behaviour (spec §7.0, §9)

- **Tap first, drag optional.** Movable things work by tapping the thing and then the destination, or by dragging. A drag that slips off leaves the item picked up, so one tap finishes it. Near misses within 60 px count.
- **Idle hint.** After 5 seconds without a touch, a ghost hand taps one good first thing once, then stays quiet for 30 seconds.
- **No failure states.** There are no scores, timers, stars, streaks or unlocks. Wrong guesses get delight ("Oh, surprise!").
- **Rooms with one activity open it directly** (§10). The app reopens on the map.
- **Spoken words** use the device's built-in voice for numbers, letters, names, colours and short hints. The grown-ups area has On / Names only / Off. The spec asks for recorded voice lines; the device voice stands in until those exist.
- **Calm Mode** shows only Cozy Nook, the three art tools and the xylophone (§10).
- **Session memory.** What she makes stays while she moves between places; reset (the broom at the top right) clears one activity. Reloading starts fresh.

## Not yet built

- Recorded voice, letter sounds and animal samples (§9.7, §13.1). Everything is synthesized.
- The wind-down timer and the Goodnight ending (§13.5). The Gallery and saving art to the device (D1). Recently played list (§11.7).
- Palm rejection and forgiving hit padding beyond the 60 px drop tolerance (§9.1–9.2).
- Release 2 and later activities, and playtesting with Layla (§19.3). The mechanics here are the research's hypotheses until she plays them.
